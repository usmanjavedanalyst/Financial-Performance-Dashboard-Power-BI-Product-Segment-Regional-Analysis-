## 📌 Overview  
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze financial performance across **products, regions, and customer segments**, identifying key drivers of revenue and profitability.

---

## 📊 Dataset  
- **Source:** Microsoft Financial Sample  
- **Industry:** Finance / Sales Analytics  
- **Dimensions:** Products, Countries, Segments, Discounts  
- **Focus Metrics:** Revenue, Profit, Margin  

---

## 📈 Key Insights  

- 📦 **Product Performance Gap**  
  - *Paseo & VTT* → highest revenue contributors  
  - *Carretera & Montana* → noticeably lower profit margins  
  ➤ High sales volume does **not guarantee profitability**

- 🌍 **Regional Profitability Differences**  
  - *United States & Germany* → strong revenue performance  
  - *France & Mexico* → lower margins despite consistent sales  
  ➤ Indicates **regional pricing / cost inefficiencies**

- 🧑‍💼 **Segment-Level Value**  
  - *Enterprise & Government* → highest profit contribution  
  - *Small Business & Midmarket* → lower margins  
  ➤ Profit is concentrated in **high-value segments**

- 💸 **Discount Impact**  
  - Higher discount products (*Montana, Carretera*) → reduced margins  
  ➤ Clear trade-off between **volume vs profitability**

- 📅 **Seasonal Trends**  
  - Revenue shows **periodic peaks across months**  
  - Products like *Paseo & Amarilla* drive peak demand  
  ➤ Demand is **time-dependent and cyclical**

---

## 🧠 Business Impact  
- Identified **profit leakage due to aggressive discounting**  
- Highlighted **high-value customer segments for targeting**  
- Exposed **underperforming regions for optimization**  
- Enabled **data-driven pricing and sales strategies**

## 🚀 Recommendations  
- Optimize pricing for **high-revenue but low-margin products**  
- Focus on **Enterprise & Government segments**  
- Apply **controlled discount strategies**  
- Improve pricing efficiency in **France & Mexico**  
- Align operations with **seasonal demand patterns**  

---

## 📌 Conclusion  
This project demonstrates how business intelligence tools like Power BI can uncover **hidden profitability issues**, showing that:

- Revenue alone is **not a reliable performance indicator**  
- Profitability depends on **pricing, discounts, segments, and regions**  
- Data-driven insights can significantly improve **business decision-making**
